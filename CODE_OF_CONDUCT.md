# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project. Participate
with empathy, assume good faith, discuss ideas rather than people, and respect
different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or serious
misconduct. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented
privately.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be handled with discretion; maintainers who are involved in a report must
recuse themselves from its resolution.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
